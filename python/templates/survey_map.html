Behind the estimate

RSSI = A_radio − 10 × n × log10(max(distance, floor))

The algorithm searches a local metre grid, solves each radio's power offset analytically, and refines multiple candidate minima. Diamonds show alternate path-loss exponents and eligible individual-radio fits. Leave-one-out points show what changes when one measurement is removed. These are sensitivity checks, not a probability distribution.

    Method: RSSI-based access point localization. Mapping: Leaflet. Local geographic data, when embedded: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. No raster tiles are requested. The local excerpt is limited in extent and detail; streets outside it are not supplied. Data, controls, diagnostics and the survey grid work offline.
